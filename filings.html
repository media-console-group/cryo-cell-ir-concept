08.3

What each form is

Plain meanings of the forms on this page.

10-K
The annual report: business, risk factors, audited financial statements. The latest covers the year ended November 30, 2025.
10-Q
The quarterly report, with unaudited statements. The latest covers the quarter ended August 31, 2026.
8-K
A current report of a specific event, such as the listing notice or a change to the credit agreement.
DEF 14A and ARS
The proxy statement for a stockholder meeting, and the annual report sent to holders with it.
SC 13D and 13D/A
Filed by an investor who holds more than 5% and may seek to influence the company, and its amendments. These are third-party filings.
