04.5

Deferred revenue and the deficit

Total liabilities exceed total assets. The largest liability is prepaid storage (deferred revenue).

What deferred revenue is. Cash the company has billed or collected for storage it has not yet provided. As it provides the storage it recognises the amount as revenue and the liability falls, without cash leaving. It is not cash on hand and it is not profit. Company statement 10-K, revenue recognition

What is inside the liabilities. Of $78,697,227 at August 31, 2026:

04.7

Debt and covenants

One lender, two instruments, two financial covenants.

Lender and facility
Susser Bank, under a Credit Agreement dated July 18, 2022: an unsecured revolving credit facility of up to $10,000,000 and a term loan of $8,960,000. Filed 10-K, Liquidity
Fifth Amendment, Oct 18, 2025
Reduced the revolving commitment from $10,000,000 to $8,000,000, extended its maturity to Oct 18, 2027 and the term note’s to Jul 29, 2032, and made the subsidiary Celle Corp. a guarantor that signed a Security Agreement. Filed 8-K
Covenants
A leverage ratio of no more than 3.50 to 1.00 and a debt service coverage ratio of at least 1.25 to 1.00, each tested at the end of every fiscal quarter on the four quarters then ended. Filed 10-K, Note 4
Balances
Line of credit: $2,300,000 at November 30, 2025 and $0 at August 31, 2026. Term note: $199,184 current plus $8,040,405 non-current at August 31, 2026. Filed
Interest rates
At November 30, 2025: 7.21% on the term note and 6.58% on the revolver. Filed

The November 2024 dividend was “funded by a revolving line of credit from Susser Bank.” Filed 10-K, Item 5 · Dividends
